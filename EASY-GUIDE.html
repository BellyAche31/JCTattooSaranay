Running Your Website
The Easy Guide

Five short steps. No coding. Take it one card at a time.

12345
1

Make your free account

Like signing up for Facebook — but for your website.

  • Go to github.com/signup

  • Enter your email, pick a username and password

  • Check your email and confirm it

Write down your username and password. This account is how you own your website — keep it safe.

2

Get the website handed to you

Two people, two small clicks.

  • Send your username to the person giving you the site

  • They press a button on their end to send it to you

  • You get an email — click Accept

That's it — the website is now 100% yours.

3

Turn the website ON

One time only. About 2 minutes.

  • Open your website page on GitHub → click Settings

  • Click Pages on the left side

  • Choose main, then click Save

  • Wait 2 minutes, then refresh the page

Your website is now live! This is its address — share it anywhere:

https://yourusername.github.io/JCTattooSaranay/
4

Make changes anytime

Prices, hours, contact info, and photos.

  • Open the file named data.js

  • Click the little pencil icon to edit

  • Only change the words between the quote marks — leave everything else alone

  • Click the green Commit changes button to save

  • Wait 1 minute — your website updates by itself

Adding photos: open the portfolio folder → Add file → Upload files → drag your photos in → Commit changes.

Made a mistake? Click Commits (clock icon) → find it → click ··· → Revert. Nothing is ever lost forever.
